AI that thinks and acts according to what it senses.

How can we expand what AI can sense, and how should its intelligence adapt to what it senses?

The physical world never reveals itself through one modality. Cameras see part of the kitchen; gaze shows where her attention is, audio hears the kettle, LiDAR maps 3D space.

Toggle the sensors to see what each one adds.

The agent fuses this evidence and adapts how it reasons to it, and also recognizes when perception is not enough: the medicine bottle is hidden.

So it acts to get the evidence it needs: it moves for a clearer view, confirms the bottle by touch, and proactively alerts her. Each action reshapes what it perceives next.
